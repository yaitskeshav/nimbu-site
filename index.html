FAQ

Good questions.

How does Nimbu know what I spent?

Your bank sends an SMS for every UPI payment, card swipe and credit. Once you turn on Auto-capture, Nimbu reads those alerts on your phone and adds each one as a transaction. You can also import past messages for a period you choose; you review everything before it's saved.

Which banks does it work with?

More than 30 banks in India, including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, AU, Bank of Baroda, Yes Bank and IndusInd. If a message isn't recognised, paste it into Nimbu once and it learns that format for next time.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. There's no Nimbu server and no account. Your data stays in the app on your phone. It only leaves if you back it up, either to a file you save or to your own Google Drive, and those backups are encrypted with your password. Read the privacy policy for details.

What does Nimbu use the internet for?

Three things: downloading daily mutual fund NAVs from AMFI, fetching crypto prices from CoinGecko, and Google Drive backups if you turn them on. There are no ads and no analytics.

What happens when I change phones?

Back up to a file or to Google Drive, install Nimbu on the new phone and choose “I have a backup”. Transactions, accounts, budgets, investments, receipts and settings all come back. You'll need your backup password; without it, the backup can't be opened.

Can I add cash spends too?

Yes. Tap + to add one by hand, use the mic to say it out loud, or import a CSV or Excel file. You can attach a receipt photo or PDF to any transaction.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Nimbu is Android-only because iOS doesn't let apps read SMS, which is what makes auto-capture possible.
