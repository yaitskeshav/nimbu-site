7. Accuracy: please double-check

Nimbu interprets bank messages automatically. Banks change their message formats, messages can arrive late or not at all, and a message can be misread. As a result a transaction may be missing, duplicated, in the wrong category or show the wrong amount.

Balances, budgets, insights, forecasts (“at this pace…”) and investment values are estimates based on what's in the app. Fund NAVs come from AMFI and crypto prices from CoinGecko; these may be delayed, unavailable or incorrect, and stock and gold values are whatever you enter.

Nimbu is a helpful tool, not an official record. Don't rely on it alone for financial, tax, legal or investment decisions; check your bank, card and fund statements, and consult a qualified professional where it matters.
